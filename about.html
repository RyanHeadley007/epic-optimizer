Who I Am

Insurance Operator
Turned Specialist.

Ryan Headley brings over two decades of hands-on insurance industry experience to every engagement — not as a distant consultant, but as someone who has lived every side of the operation.

He began his career managing operations at a flight school before entering the insurance industry in 2003. That early experience instilled a precision-minded approach to operations that has defined his work ever since.

As the owner-operator of Stratton Agency in San Carlos, CA, Ryan built and led an independent insurance agency from the ground up — navigating the daily realities of client relationships, staff management, carrier negotiations, and the technology that holds it all together. When he sold the agency to PCF Insurance in 2018, it was a testament to what he built.

Following the sale, Ryan moved into specialized consulting, focusing on the Applied Epic management system — the platform most agencies rely on and few fully leverage. That expertise led to his partnership with Paul Hammack at Panorama starting in March 2020, and eventually to founding Epic Optimizer as a dedicated practice built around one discipline done exceptionally well.
