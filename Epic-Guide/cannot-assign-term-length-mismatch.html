Cannot Assign: 6 Month Term
but 12 Month in Epic

Resolve a download blocked from assignment because the policy term in the download (6 months) doesn't match the term entered in Epic (12 months). Covers identifying the mismatch, correcting the effective and expiration dates at the policy level, and completing the assignment.

Scenario

A download arrives for a policy, but the transaction cannot be assigned. The root cause is a term length mismatch: the policy was entered in Epic with a 12-month term, but the carrier's download reflects a 6-month term. Because the effective date range in Epic doesn't match the downloaded transaction's effective date range, the assignment is blocked. Since the policy is still "Not Issued" and "In-Process," the standard Change Effective Dates function from the suspense screen will not work — the correction must be made at the client/policy level first.

Phase 01 Suspense Item Selection
1
Highlight the transaction in Interface Suspense and click Find.
Locate the download sitting in suspense that is failing to assign.
2
Review the key data fields on the download transaction.
Before attempting assignment, confirm the following from the download detail:
  • Effective Date Range (e.g., 6-month vs. 12-month term)
  • Transaction Type
  • Transaction Date
  • Line Type
Alert: If the Effective Date Range on the download does not match the term in Epic, assignment will fail. In this scenario, Epic shows an annual (12-month) term while the download reflects a 6-month term.
3
Note the limitation before proceeding.
Because the policy is still Not Issued and In-Process in Epic, the "Change Effective Dates" option from the suspense assignment screen will not work. The correction must be made directly on the policy record at the client/account level.
Tip: This is a key distinction from the Transaction Date Mismatch workflow. That scenario allows a date correction from within the suspense screen. This scenario requires navigating out to the policy record itself.
Phase 02 Client Account Lookup
4
Click Locate to search for the specific account.
Use the account lookup to navigate to the client record associated with the download.
5
Select Policies from the sidebar menu.
Once inside the client account, navigate to the Policies section to locate the relevant policy record.
Phase 03 Policy Date Adjustment
6
Locate the policy with the incorrect expiration date.
Identify the policy in the list that reflects the 12-month term. The expiration date will need to be corrected to match the 6-month term from the download.
7
Open the Actions menu and select Change Effective/Expiration Dates.
This option is available at the policy level (unlike the suspense screen, which is blocked for Not Issued/In-Process policies).
8
Enter the updated Effective and Expiration dates, then click Finish.
Use the dates from the download transaction to determine the correct 6-month expiration date. Update both fields as needed and confirm the change.
Important: Double-check the downloaded transaction's effective date range before entering these values. The corrected expiration date in Epic must match exactly.
Phase 04 Suspense Item Assignment
9
Return to Interface Suspense and select the policy from the results.
Navigate back to the suspense item. The date correction has now been applied at the policy level.
10
Click Find to refresh the policy results.
11
Verify the policy expiration date now matches the downloaded transaction's effective date range.
Confirm the policy in the results list reflects the corrected 6-month term before proceeding with assignment.
12
Click Select Existing.
13
Click Update Existing to apply the download to the policy.
14
Click Yes on the confirmation prompt.
15
Verify the lookup code was assigned.
Confirm that the account lookup code now appears on the policy record, indicating the download has been successfully assigned.
