Scenario

A download arrives with a PCH (policy change) transaction. You can locate the policy in Epic, but only the prior term is found. The download is for an endorsement to the current term — but in order to attach an endorsement, the policy must first be issued. This guide walks through renewing the policy to the matching term, issuing it, and then assigning the PCH transaction from Interface Suspense.

Phase 01 Policy & Account Search
1
Select the transaction you want to process.
2
Inspect the details of the download.
  • Effective Dates
  • Transaction Type
  • Transaction Effective Date
  • Line Type
3
Click Find to search for policies.
4
Verify policy details found in Epic.
Alert: When you search, you may only find the prior term (e.g. 25–26) in Epic, but the download is a PCH to the current term (e.g. 26–27). Proceed to locate the policy directly in Epic to check its status.
5
Copy the policy number into the Epic Search area.
Make sure you are searching by Policy #.
6
Click Locate.
7
Click Policies.
8
Review the policy list to verify the downloaded term does not yet exist.
Phase 02 Policy Renewal Process
9
Open the Actions menu and select Renew.
10
Click Finish and confirm the renewal changes by clicking OK.
Phase 03 Activity & Commission Management
11
Set the activity status to Closed and click Finish.
12
Handle the PR/BR screen if redirected.
If redirected to the PR/BR screen, check the expiring policy and update accordingly. If no commission agreement is available or the number changes, continue — the agency will clean up. Close out the policy and leave as In-Process.
13
Select Actions > Issue/Not Issue Policy.
Phase 04 Suspense Item Assignment
14
Switch to the Procedures tab.
15
Select the policy row in the Procedures tab and click Find.
16
Click Select Existing, then Update Existing, and confirm the update.
17
Confirm the account lookup code has been added.
