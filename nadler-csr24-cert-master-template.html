Before You Start

Certificate master templates are reusable starting points for frequently issued certificates — they store the holder, additional insured language, and policy selections so you don't rebuild them each time. You will need access to CSR24 and the account must already have active policies in Applied Epic for the template to reference. Confirm the correct policy lines are active before beginning.

Account & Policy ↓ Template Setup ↓ Save & Reuse ↓
Phase 01 Account & Policy Selection
1
Open the account in CSR24
Navigate to the insured's account in CSR24. Templates are account-specific — a master template created here will only be available for certificates issued under this account.
2
Go to Certificates and select Master Templates
From the account view, navigate to the Certificates section and select the Master Templates option. This is where existing templates are listed and new ones are created.
Tip: If you don't see a Master Templates option, confirm your CSR24 user group has permissions for template management. Not all user roles include this capability.
3
Click "Add New Template"
Select the option to create a new master template. A template creation form will open where you will configure all the reusable certificate details.
4
Name the template
Give the template a clear, descriptive name that will make sense to anyone issuing certificates from this account in the future. A good naming convention includes the holder name or the use case.
Example naming conventions: "Standard GL – ABC General Contractor" or "Renewal Batch – Commercial Auto". Vague names like "Template 1" create confusion when multiple templates exist on the same account.
5
Select the certificate form type
Choose the appropriate certificate form — typically ACORD 25 for liability or ACORD 28 for property. The form selection determines which policy fields and coverage sections appear in the template.
Important: The form type cannot be changed after the template is saved. If you select the wrong form, you will need to delete the template and start over. Confirm with the holder or your account manager which form is required before proceeding.
Phase 02 Template Configuration
6
Enter the certificate holder information
Complete the holder name and address fields. This is the entity that will appear in the "Certificate Holder" box on the issued certificate. Accuracy here is critical — the holder name and address must match what the contract or holder requires exactly.
Watch for: Holders sometimes specify a very particular address format or legal entity name. When in doubt, ask the insured to provide the holder's required language rather than guessing.
7
Select the policies to include
Check the boxes for each policy line that should appear on the certificate. Only active policies tied to this account in Applied Epic will be available for selection.
  • General Liability — most commonly required
  • Auto Liability — required when vehicles are involved in the contract
  • Workers' Compensation — required for most construction and service contracts
  • Umbrella / Excess — include when the holder specifies minimum limits that require umbrella support
Only include what the holder requires. Including extra policy lines on a certificate that weren't requested can create unnecessary questions or confusion for the holder. If the contract says GL and Auto only, select GL and Auto only.
8
Add Additional Insured language (if required)
If the holder requires Additional Insured status, enter the endorsement language in the Description of Operations field or the designated Additional Insured section, depending on your CSR24 setup. This language carries over to every certificate issued from this template.
Use endorsed language only. Do not write custom AI language — only include language that is supported by an actual endorsement on the policy. If the policy does not have a Blanket Additional Insured endorsement, the language cannot legitimately appear on the certificate. Confirm with the underwriter or policy paperwork first.
9
Complete the Description of Operations
Enter any project-specific or contract-required language in the Description of Operations / Locations / Vehicles field. Common entries include project names, job numbers, waiver of subrogation notices, and primary & non-contributory language.
Keep it clean: Only enter language that was specifically requested. Overloaded Description of Operations fields are a common source of certificate complaints — if something wasn't asked for, leave it out.
Phase 03 Save & Reuse the Template
10
Review all entries before saving
Before clicking Save, review the holder name and address, the selected policies, AI language, and Description of Operations. Templates get reused — any error saved here will repeat on every certificate issued from this template until someone catches it and fixes it.
11
Click "Save" to store the template
Save the template. It will now appear in the Master Templates list for this account and can be selected as a starting point any time a certificate needs to be issued to this holder.
Confirm it saved: After saving, verify the template appears in the list with the correct name. If the save fails or the template doesn't appear, do not assume it saved — reattempt before leaving the screen.
12
Issue a certificate from the template
When a certificate request comes in for this holder, select the saved master template from the account's template list. The holder details, policy selections, and Description of Operations will prepopulate — you can adjust any field before issuing without changing the saved template.
Templates do not auto-update policy limits. When a policy renews with new limits, CSR24 pulls the current policy data from Applied Epic — so the certificate will reflect the updated limits. However, if the policy number itself changes or a policy is replaced, review the template to confirm the correct policies are still selected.
Common Questions
Can I edit a master template after it's been saved? ▾

Yes — master templates can be edited at any time from the Master Templates list. Open the template, make your changes, and save. The update will apply to all future certificates issued from that template. Certificates already issued are not retroactively affected. Note that the certificate form type (ACORD 25, ACORD 28, etc.) cannot be changed after the initial save — if the wrong form was selected, you will need to delete the template and create a new one.

How many master templates can I create per account? ▾

CSR24 does not impose a hard cap on the number of templates per account in most configurations. However, accounts with a large number of templates can become difficult to manage — the template list does not have robust filtering, so finding the right template requires scrolling. Keep templates organized with consistent naming conventions, and archive or delete any templates that are no longer in use to keep the list manageable.

What happens to a template when a policy renews? ▾

When a policy renews in Applied Epic and the policy number stays the same (a rewrite of the same policy), CSR24 will pull the updated policy data — effective dates, limits, and carrier information — automatically when you issue a certificate from the template. If the renewal results in a new policy number (a new policy replacing the old one), the template may still be pointing at the old policy number. In that case, open the template, remove the old policy, add the new one, and re-save before the next certificate is issued from it.

Can the same template be used across multiple accounts? ▾

No — master templates in CSR24 are account-specific. A template created under Account A is only available when issuing certificates for Account A. If the same holder requires certificates from multiple accounts, you will need to create a separate template under each account. There is no global or cross-account template library in CSR24.

The holder address changed — do I update the template or the certificate? ▾

Update the template. If you only update the individual certificate, the change will apply to that one issuance but the next certificate from the template will still have the old address. Open the master template, correct the holder address, save, and then issue the certificate. That way every future certificate from that template will have the correct address without needing a manual correction each time.
