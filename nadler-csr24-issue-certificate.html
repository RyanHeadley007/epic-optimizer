How to Issue a
Certificate of Insurance in CSR24

Step-by-step walkthrough for issuing a Certificate of Insurance through CSR24 — from locating the account and selecting the correct policy lines through delivery and confirmation.

CSR24

Before You Start

Certificate issuance in CSR24 begins from the insured's account — confirm the account is open and the correct policy lines are active in Applied Epic before proceeding. The certificate holder's name and address must be on hand, and you should know whether any additional insured language or special Description of Operations language is required by the holder's contract before you begin.

Account & Policy ↓ Certificate Setup ↓ Review & Deliver ↓
Phase 01 Account & Policy Selection
1
Open the account in CSR24
Navigate to the insured's account in CSR24. All certificate issuance is account-specific — you must be on the correct account before proceeding, as the policies and contacts available will be tied to that account only.
2
Navigate to Certificates
From the account view, select the Certificates section. This is where all certificate activity for the account lives — both existing certificates and the option to issue new ones.
3
Click "Issue New Certificate"
Select the option to issue a new certificate. If the account has saved master templates, you will be prompted to either start from a template or build from scratch. Starting from a template is faster when one exists for this holder — the holder info and policy selections will prepopulate.
No template for this holder? Select the blank certificate option and complete all fields manually. Consider saving a master template afterward if this holder is likely to request certificates regularly.
4
Select the certificate form type
Choose the appropriate ACORD form for the certificate being issued:
  • ACORD 25 — Certificate of Liability Insurance (most common)
  • ACORD 28 — Evidence of Commercial Property Insurance
  • ACORD 27 — Evidence of Personal Property Insurance
Confirm the required form before selecting. The holder's contract or request will specify which form they need. Issuing the wrong form type means the certificate will be rejected and you will need to reissue.
5
Select the policies to include
Check the boxes for each policy line the holder has requested. Only active policies tied to this account in Applied Epic will be available.
  • General Liability
  • Auto Liability
  • Workers' Compensation
  • Umbrella / Excess
Only include what was requested. Adding unrequested policy lines creates confusion for the holder and may raise questions the insured hasn't authorized you to answer on their behalf.
Phase 02 Certificate Setup
6
Enter the certificate holder information
Complete the holder name and full mailing address. This is the entity that will appear in the Certificate Holder box on the issued certificate. The name and address must match exactly what the holder's contract specifies.
Exact match required. Holders frequently reject certificates when the legal entity name or address doesn't match their contract. When in doubt, ask the insured to provide the holder's required name and address verbatim rather than guessing.
7
Add Additional Insured language if required
If the holder requires Additional Insured status, confirm the policy has the appropriate endorsement — Blanket Additional Insured or a specifically scheduled endorsement — before adding the language. Enter the endorsed language in the designated Additional Insured or Description of Operations section.
Never add AI language without an endorsement. If the policy does not carry the endorsement, the language cannot legitimately appear on the certificate. Verify against the policy paperwork or confirm with the underwriter first.
8
Complete the Description of Operations
Enter any project, contract, or holder-required language in the Description of Operations / Locations / Vehicles field. Common entries include:
  • Project name or job number
  • Waiver of Subrogation notice
  • Primary & Non-Contributory language
  • Additional Insured endorsement reference
Less is more. Only enter what the holder specifically requested. Certificates with overloaded Description of Operations fields create unnecessary questions. If it wasn't asked for, leave it out.
9
Verify the insured's contact information
Confirm the Named Insured name and address at the top of the certificate are correct. This information pulls from the Applied Epic account record — if it appears wrong, the fix should be made at the source in Epic, not directly on the certificate.
Phase 03 Review & Deliver
10
Preview the certificate before issuing
Use the preview function to review the completed certificate before it is issued. Check every field — holder name and address, policy effective and expiration dates, coverage limits, and Description of Operations — before moving forward.
Preview is your last check. Once the certificate is issued, it becomes a document of record. Correcting an error means voiding the original and reissuing, which can delay the insured's project or contract.
11
Set the delivery method and recipient
Select how the certificate will be delivered. Options typically include:
  • Email — enter the holder's email address; the certificate is sent as a PDF attachment
  • Download / Print — generate a PDF to send manually or hand-deliver
Confirm the delivery email address. Sending to the wrong address means the holder never receives the certificate — and you may not know until they follow up. When the address is unfamiliar, verify it with the insured before sending.
12
Issue the certificate
Click Issue to finalize and send. The certificate is now a document of record under this account in CSR24 and will appear in the account's certificate history. A copy is also logged in Applied Epic against the account.
Confirm it issued: After clicking Issue, verify the certificate appears in the account's certificate list with the correct issue date and holder name. If it doesn't appear, do not assume it sent — check for an error and reissue if necessary before notifying the insured that it's been sent.
Common Questions
The policy limits shown on the certificate don't look right — what do I do? ▾

CSR24 pulls policy data directly from Applied Epic, so if the limits look wrong on the certificate, the issue is almost certainly in the Epic policy record — not in CSR24. Cancel out of the certificate, go to the policy in Applied Epic, confirm the limits are entered correctly, save, and then reopen the certificate in CSR24. The updated data should pull through. Do not manually override limits on the certificate — the data needs to be correct at the source.

Can I issue a certificate without a holder email address? ▾

Yes — select the Download or Print delivery option instead of Email. This generates a PDF you can send manually, upload to a portal, or hand off to the insured to forward themselves. You are not required to send directly from CSR24 to the holder.

I issued the certificate with an error — can I edit it? ▾

Issued certificates cannot be edited directly in CSR24. To correct an error, you need to void or supersede the original certificate and issue a corrected replacement. The replacement will have a new certificate number. If the holder already received the original, notify them that a corrected version is being sent and ask them to disregard the prior one. This is why the preview step matters — catching errors before issue avoids the voiding process entirely.

Does the insured get a copy automatically when a certificate is issued? ▾

That depends on how the account's CSR24 settings are configured. The Send a copy of all certificates to this insured field in the account settings controls whether the insured is automatically CC'd on every certificate issued. If it is turned on, the insured will receive a copy every time. If the account is also used for renewal batch processing, review this setting carefully before running the batch — it can result in a large volume of certificate copies being sent to the insured at once if left on.

Where can I find a certificate after it has been issued? ▾

Issued certificates appear in the account's certificate history in CSR24 — accessible from the Certificates section of the account. From there you can view, re-send, void, or use the issued certificate as a starting point for a new one. The certificate is also logged in the Applied Epic account record, so it is visible from within Epic as well.
