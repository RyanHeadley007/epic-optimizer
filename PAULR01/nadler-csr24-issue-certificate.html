Certificate of Insurance in CSR24

Step-by-step walkthrough for issuing a Certificate of Insurance through CSR24 — from locating the account and selecting the correct policy lines through delivery and confirmation.

Before You Start

This workflow covers navigating Applied Epic and generating a Certificate of Insurance for a commercial account in CSR24. It covers selecting templates, adding policy and holder information, setting delivery preferences, and confirming the certificate synced back to Epic. Have the holder's name, address, and any contract requirements on hand before you begin.

Access CSR24 ↓ Issue Certificate ↓ Select / Add Holder ↓ Delivery & Submit ↓ Epic Integration ↓
Phase 01 Access CSR24 to Issue Certificate
1
Start on the Client home screen in Epic
Open the insured's account and confirm you are on the Client home screen in Applied Epic before proceeding.
2
Navigate to Certificates via Proofs of Insurance
From the Client screen, click Proofs of Insurance → Premium Processing → Certificates. This will launch CSR24 and land you on the client home screen within the portal.
Phase 02 Issue a New Certificate
3
Click "Issue Cert"
From the CSR24 client home screen, click the Issue Cert button. You will be redirected to the Issue a Certificate screen, which displays a list of available master templates for this account.
4
Check for red templates before proceeding
Review the template list before selecting anything.
Alert: If any template is displayed in RED, that means the template has expired due to a policy being out of its effective date range. Do not issue a certificate from a red template. See your direct support contact for more information.
5
Select the correct template based on holder requirements
Based on what the holder is requesting, select the appropriate template. For example, if the holder needs to be listed as an Additional Insured for General Liability, select the "25/26 Master w/ AI endt" template.
No Template for this Holder? Stop the process. All certificates must be issued off a template. Contact your manager for clarification. If directed to create a new template, use the Create a Master Template workflow before proceeding.
Select the correct template — attachments are assigned at the template level. If you select the AI template, a copy of the AI endorsement will automatically be attached to the certificate after issuance.
6
Verify limits in the template view
After selecting a template, you will be taken to the template view where you can confirm the limits are correct and that you have the right template. Do not worry about the Additional Insured or Waiver of Subrogation selections here — you will update those per holder in the next steps.
Phase 03 Select / Add Holder
7
Click "Select Holder" — always search first
Click Select Holder and search the existing list before assuming a holder is new. Even if you think the holder is new, always start here to avoid creating a duplicate holder on the account. If they are not found on the list, you can add them from the next screen.
8
Add the holder if not found
If the holder does not appear in the list, click Add Holder. Enter the holder's full legal name and mailing address exactly as required by their contract.
Exact match required. Holders frequently reject certificates when the legal name or address doesn't match their contract. When in doubt, ask the insured to provide the holder's required information verbatim.
9
Consider the "One Time Only" option
The One Time Only selection is used when the certificate holder only needs to be named once and will not require a renewal certificate. This is typically used for special events and short-term policies. If selecting this option, set the Date Interest Ends to the day after the contract ends.
10
Check "Do they Receive Renewals?" if applicable
If this is not a one-time certificate and the holder will require renewals, check the Do they Receive Renewals? box. In the delivery section, you will be able to elect to send those renewals via email automatically.
11
Enter the number of Cancellation Days
Enter the required number of cancellation notice days for this holder. The standard is typically 30 days unless the holder's contract specifies otherwise.
12
Set Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation at the certificate line level
This is where you tell the certificate whether to include the holder as Additional Insured and/or Waiver of Subrogation on each policy line. Only mark Y for what the holder specifically requested.
Example: If the holder needs Additional Insured on GL only and did not request Waiver of Subrogation on GL or Workers' Comp, mark: GL Additional Insured = Y, GL Subrogation Waiver = N, WC Subrogation Waiver = N.
13
Review the Endorsements section
The Endorsements section allows you to attach additional documents to the certificate. If the AI endorsement was already added at the template level, you do not need to add it again here. Use this section only when you need to bring in additional attachments that are not already on the template.
14
Complete the Description of Operations — Holder Specific Portion
There are two boxes in the Description of Operations section. The Certificate Portion pulls from the template level and applies to all holders — it is hardcoded and cannot be updated here. The Holder Specific Portion is where you do your work for this individual holder.
Use Library Lookup to access the agency's approved Description of Operations wording. Select the corresponding language based on the certificate requirements rather than typing it manually, to ensure approved language is used consistently.
Phase 04 Delivery Recipient Setup & Distribution
15
Review the Delivery section options
The delivery section allows you to send copies to up to 3 additional recipients. Note the following rules:
  • A copy will automatically be delivered to the Servicing Role CSR on file and an activity will be created in Epic with a copy attached
  • You may send to up to 3 additional parties
  • Do not enter the client's email in this section — delivery to the client is controlled elsewhere
16
Decide on the "Send Me Confirmation" option
A copy will automatically be delivered to the Servicing Role CSR on file and an activity will be created in Epic. Unless you specifically want to receive a separate email copy yourself, it is recommended to leave the Send Me Confirmation option unchecked.
17
Select "Supersedes Prior Certificate" if applicable
If this certificate is replacing a previously issued certificate, select the Supersedes Prior Certificate option. This links the new certificate to the one it is replacing in the system record.
18
Complete Recipient #1 — always use the holder's delivery email
Always enter the holder's delivery email in Recipient #1 when available. Complete all fields:
  • Attention — name of the person you are sending to
  • Subject Line — keep it vague and do not include a year, as this will be reused at renewal (e.g., "Proof of Insurance for [Company Name]")
  • Message — keep it generic for the same reason; the message will be reused for all future certificates to this holder
Example message: "Hello, Please find the attached Certificate of Insurance for your records. If you have any questions or require additional information, please do not hesitate to reach out. Thank you, Certificate Processing Team – [Agency Name]"
19
Set email distribution options per recipient
For each recipient, select which certificates they should receive:
  • Email Current Certificate — sends the certificate being issued right now
  • Email Renewal Cert — uses the same subject line, message, and email address for all future certificates to this holder, including renewals
When in doubt, always check "Email Renewal Cert." If the holder is One Time Only, you do not need to check this since they will not receive future certificates. For all other holders, selecting this now maximizes automation at renewal time.
20
Enter the holder's email address for Recipient #1
Enter the holder's email address in the Recipient #1 email field. If additional recipients are required, complete Recipient #2 and Recipient #3 following the same steps.
Confirm the email address. Sending to the wrong address means the holder never receives the certificate. Verify any unfamiliar address with the insured before submitting.
21
Click Preview, then Submit
Click Preview to review the completed certificate before it is issued — confirm the holder name and address, policy dates, limits, and Description of Operations are all correct. Once satisfied, click Submit. The Delivery Information screen will display showing all recipients who were emailed a copy.
Preview is your last check. Issued certificates cannot be edited. Catching an error before Submit avoids the voiding and reissuing process entirely.
Phase 05 Epic Integration Details
22
Informational: what happens in Epic after Submit
No action is required in this phase — it is for informational purposes only. Updates are written to Epic immediately upon submission. Return to the account in Epic to confirm the certificate synced correctly.
Good to know: CSR24 writes the certificate data back to Epic immediately on submission. You do not need to manually log or attach the certificate in Epic — the sync handles it.
23
Return to Epic and open the Activity section
Navigate back to the account in Epic and select the Activity section. Click Activities → All to see all activity records for the account.
24
Locate the 4CER activity created by CSR24
Sort the activity list by Entered On Date (most recent first) and look for the 4CER activity code. This is the activity automatically created by CSR24 when it syncs to Epic upon certificate submission.
25
Access the activity and view the attached certificate PDF
Highlight the 4CER activity, click Access, then click Attachments. The activity will display the PDF copy of the issued certificate. Double-click to open it or view it in the preview pane to confirm the correct certificate is attached.
Common Questions
The policy limits shown on the certificate don't look right — what do I do? ▾

CSR24 pulls policy data directly from Applied Epic, so if the limits look wrong on the certificate, the issue is almost certainly in the Epic policy record — not in CSR24. Cancel out of the certificate, go to the policy in Applied Epic, confirm the limits are entered correctly, save, and then reopen the certificate in CSR24. The updated data should pull through. Do not manually override limits on the certificate — the data needs to be correct at the source.

Can I issue a certificate without a holder email address? ▾

Yes — select the Download or Print delivery option instead of Email. This generates a PDF you can send manually, upload to a portal, or hand off to the insured to forward themselves. You are not required to send directly from CSR24 to the holder.

I issued the certificate with an error — can I edit it? ▾

Issued certificates cannot be edited directly in CSR24. To correct an error, you need to void or supersede the original certificate and issue a corrected replacement. The replacement will have a new certificate number. If the holder already received the original, notify them that a corrected version is being sent and ask them to disregard the prior one. This is why the preview step matters — catching errors before issue avoids the voiding process entirely.

Does the insured get a copy automatically when a certificate is issued? ▾

That depends on how the account's CSR24 settings are configured. The Send a copy of all certificates to this insured field in the account settings controls whether the insured is automatically CC'd on every certificate issued. If it is turned on, the insured will receive a copy every time. If the account is also used for renewal batch processing, review this setting carefully before running the batch — it can result in a large volume of certificate copies being sent to the insured at once if left on.

Where can I find a certificate after it has been issued? ▾

Issued certificates appear in the account's certificate history in CSR24 — accessible from the Certificates section of the account. From there you can view, re-send, void, or use the issued certificate as a starting point for a new one. The certificate is also logged in the Applied Epic account record, so it is visible from within Epic as well.
