How to Create an
Agency Bill Invoice

Follow this guide to learn how to process and add an insurance invoice within the Epic system. Covers transaction entry, line item detail, commission verification, invoice generation, and carrier invoice attachment.

5 Phases
46 Steps
Applied Epic

Before You Start

Have the carrier invoice open on a second screen before entering anything in Epic. You will reference it for every line item, description, and the final total verification. Keep it visible throughout the entire workflow.

Phase 01 Receive the Carrier Invoice
1
Receive the carrier invoice via email
The carrier invoice arrives by email. Open the attached invoice to review all charge details.
2
Click the attached invoice to view the details
Open the attachment to review all line items, fees, taxes, and the total before entering anything into Epic.
3
Navigate back to Epic with the invoice on a second screen
Place a copy of the carrier invoice on a second screen for quick reference throughout the entire entry process.
4
Highlight the policy you are creating an invoice for
In Epic, locate and highlight the policy line that corresponds to the carrier invoice you received.
5
Click New
With the policy highlighted, click the New button to begin creating a new transaction.
6
Click "Transaction"
From the New menu, select Transaction to open the invoice entry workflow.
Phase 02 Start the Invoice
7
Check that "Policy" is selected
Verify the transaction type is set to Policy before proceeding.
8
Select the policy
Choose the correct policy from the dropdown to associate this invoice with the right policy record.
9
Add your first transaction — select the premium transaction code
Select the transaction code that matches the invoice type. Use RENB for a renewal invoice.
Transaction code reference: RENB (Renewal Business), NEWB (New Business), ENDT (Endorsement), CANC (Cancellation). Be sure to use the right transaction type for this invoice.
10
Enter the premium only
Enter the premium amount only from the carrier invoice. Do not include fees or taxes here — those are added as separate line items in the next phase.
11
Click "Continue"
Click Continue to proceed to the line item summary screen.
12
Review the premium line item
The next page displays the line item for your premium entry. Confirm the amount is correct before adding additional charges.
Phase 03 Adding Additional Line Transactions
13
Click the Add (+) button
Click the Add (+) button to enter additional transaction lines for company fees, taxes, and any other charges on the carrier invoice.
14
Select the correct transaction code from the dropdown
Using the dropdown, select the transaction code that matches the charge you are entering (e.g., CFEE for a company fee).
15
Enter the next transaction from the carrier invoice
Using the carrier invoice as your reference, enter the next charge — for example, an Inspection Fee of $100.00.
16
Update the description to match the carrier invoice
Select the CFEE line and update the Description field to match the exact label on the carrier invoice for that charge.
17
Enter the total and click "Add"
Enter the dollar amount for this line item and click Add to post it to the invoice.
18
Confirm the new line item populates in the header
The new charge will appear as a line in the invoice header. Verify the description and amount are correct before continuing.
19
Repeat for all remaining line items
Continue the Add → Code → Description → Amount process for every remaining charge on the carrier invoice.
  • Use the carrier invoice description exactly as written
  • Enter the correct charge per line item
  • Continue until all transaction lines have been entered
20
When done adding the final line, select "Finish"
Once all charges have been entered, click Finish to proceed to the total verification step.
21
Review the total on the carrier invoice
Note the total amount shown at the bottom of the carrier invoice. This is the number you will compare against Epic.
22
Check the total in Epic to make sure they match
Compare the Epic invoice total against the carrier invoice total. They must match before proceeding to the Invoice step.
Agency Fee Exception: If an Agency Fee (AFEE) is included, the Epic total will exceed the carrier invoice total by exactly the agency fee amount. This is expected — the agency is collecting an additional fee for placing this policy. The difference between the two totals should equal the AFEE amount exactly.
23
Click "Invoice"
Click the Invoice button to move to delivery method and commission settings.
24
Confirm Delivery Method is set to Mail
Verify the Delivery Method is set to Mail. Adjust only if the account has a documented exception.
25
Click "Commissions"
Click the Commissions button to verify the commission percentage for this policy.
26
Verify the line commission percent is entered
Confirm the line commission percentage is correctly entered. This is the percentage Epic withholds as revenue for the agency before remitting the balance to the carrier.
27
Click "Finish"
Click Finish. Epic will redirect you to the Transactions page.
Phase 04 Generating the Final Invoice & INVC Activity
28
You are redirected to the Transactions page
After finalizing the invoice entry, Epic redirects you to the Transactions page where you will generate the final invoice.
29
Click Actions
Click the Actions button on the Transactions page to open the available workflow options.
30
Click "Generate Invoice"
Select Generate Invoice from the Actions menu to open the Generate Invoice workflow in Epic.
31
The Generate Invoice workflow opens
Epic opens the Generate Invoice workflow. Continue through the steps below to complete distribution and finalize.
32
Click "Distribution"
Click the Distribution option to configure how the invoice will be distributed.
33
Verify Distribution is set to "Print"
Confirm the Distribution method is set to Print before finishing.
34
Click "Finish"
Click Finish. Generating an invoice is a system event — Epic will automatically create an INVC activity to track the finalization of the process.
35
The INVC activity is created
Epic creates the INVC activity automatically. This activity tracks the finalization of the invoice process and is where both invoice documents will be stored.
36
Update the Activity Description line
Update the INVC activity description to reflect the invoice details — include carrier name, policy information, and premium amount.
37
Check the Follow-up Date
Set the follow-up date to 15 days before the invoice due date. This gives the agency time to follow up on payment before the invoice becomes past due.
38
Enter a follow-up note and click Finish
Enter a brief follow-up note summarizing the invoice, then click Finish to complete the INVC activity setup.
39
A copy of the invoice will display — you can close it
A preview of the generated invoice will appear on screen. You can close this window — the attachment will automatically be attached to the INVC activity.
Note: Do not worry about saving this preview manually. Epic auto-attaches the Epic-generated invoice to the INVC activity as soon as it is closed.
Phase 05 Attach Carrier Invoice to INVC Activity
40
Click Activities
Navigate to the Activities section of the policy to locate the INVC activity that was just created.
41
Highlight the INVC Activity that was just created
Click the INVC activity to highlight it. This is the target for the carrier invoice attachment.
42
Return to the email (or desktop) with the carrier invoice
Go back to the email containing the carrier invoice, or to your desktop if you saved the file locally.
43
Drag & drop the carrier invoice into the INVC activity — click Finish
Drag the carrier invoice file and drop it directly onto the highlighted INVC activity in Epic, then click Finish.
44
Spot-check the attached documents — click "Access"
Click Access on the INVC activity to verify the documents attached. Both the Epic invoice and the carrier invoice must be present to complete the audit trail.
45
Click Attachments
Click the Attachments tab to view all documents associated with the INVC activity.
46
Verify the attachments
Confirm that both documents are present before closing the activity:
  • The Epic-generated invoice (auto-attached when the preview was closed)
  • The carrier invoice (manually drag-dropped in step 43)
Workflow complete. Both documents are attached and the audit trail is complete. See additional workflows for clearing the A/R Balance and closing the INVC activity.
Common Questions
Which premium transaction code do I use?▼

Use the code that matches the invoice type: RENB for a renewal, NEWB for new business, ENDT for an endorsement, and CANC for a cancellation. If you are unsure, check the carrier invoice — the policy transaction type is typically noted in the header or description section.

Why does my Epic total not match the carrier invoice?▼

If the totals do not match and no Agency Fee (AFEE) was added, there is likely a data entry error — a missing line item, a wrong amount, or a duplicated charge. Review each line against the carrier invoice before clicking Invoice. If an AFEE was intentionally added, the Epic total will be higher than the carrier invoice by exactly the agency fee amount — this is correct and expected.

What is the AFEE transaction code used for?▼

AFEE (Agency Fee) is used when the agency wants to collect an additional fee for placing the policy — beyond what the carrier is charging. This is separate from carrier fees (CFEE). When AFEE is added, the Epic invoice total will exceed the carrier invoice total by the agency fee amount. This difference is intentional and should be documented in the INVC activity description.

Why is the follow-up date set to 15 days before the due date?▼

The 15-day buffer gives the agency time to follow up on payment before the invoice becomes past due. When the INVC activity surfaces at 15 days out, the responsible party can contact the insured or finance company to confirm payment is on track. If payment has already been confirmed, the activity can be closed early.

Do I need to save the invoice preview that appears after clicking Finish?▼

No. The invoice preview that displays after generating the invoice is for your reference only. You can close it immediately — Epic automatically attaches a copy of the generated invoice to the INVC activity. You will confirm it is there in steps 44–46 when you verify the attachments.
