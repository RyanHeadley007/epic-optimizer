Common Questions
What is the difference between "Downloading" and "AMS Configured"? ▼

Downloading means the carrier connection is active and data is flowing into Epic — policies, transactions, and documents are being received automatically. AMS Configured means the connection has been set up in Applied Epic's system settings, but you should verify that data is actually flowing by checking recent download activity in Interface Suspense. A configured connection that isn't actively receiving data may need to be activated on the carrier's IVANS side.

What does "Available" mean — why isn't it set up? ▼

Available means the carrier offers IVANS download capability for that line of business, but Nadler Insurance has not yet configured or activated it. This could be because the volume doesn't justify the setup effort, the carrier was recently added, or it's on the list for a future configuration phase. If you believe a connection marked Available should be active, flag it for review.

What does "Downloading as Package" mean? ▼

Some carriers send Commercial Lines data as a single combined package transaction rather than as separate line-of-business downloads. "Downloading as Package" means the carrier bundles multiple coverages into one download record. This affects how the transaction appears in Interface Suspense and how it maps to policy lines in Epic — the download will show as a package type rather than individual CL lines.

What is "Want Future" and who decides when it gets configured? ▼

Want Future indicates a carrier connection that Nadler Insurance has flagged as desirable but has not yet prioritized for setup. These are typically connections where the carrier offers the capability, the agency wants it, but it hasn't been scheduled into a configuration phase yet. Decisions about when to move these forward are made during agency operations reviews — if you have a specific carrier you'd like prioritized, bring it to your Epic administrator.

Why does Nationwide appear twice? ▼

"Nationwide" and "Nationwide Insurance" are two separate IVANS connection profiles in Epic — they represent different carrier relationships or appointment types within the Nationwide family. The standard Nationwide entry is actively downloading across multiple lines. The "Nationwide Insurance" entry is flagged as Do Not Want across most lines (the agency has intentionally excluded those downloads), with the exception of Homeowners which is AMS Configured.
