Assigning Download Files
from Suspense

Once a suspense item is located, this workflow covers how to match and assign the downloaded file to the correct existing policy — from selecting the transaction through confirming the lookup code assignment.

2 Phases
9 Steps
~3 Minutes
Applied Epic
Interface Management

Before You Start

This workflow begins where Access Suspense Items leaves off — you should already be in the suspense queue with a filtered list of items for your carrier. Select the policy transaction you want to assign, then follow the steps below to match it to the correct existing policy and confirm the lookup code is properly attached.

Account Search & Filtering ↓ Policy Selection & Assignment ↓
Phase 01 Account Search and Filtering
1
Select the policy transaction from the suspense list
Starting in the suspense area of Applied Epic, click the policy transaction you need to assign. This opens the account search panel where Epic will attempt to locate a matching policy to attach the download to.
2
Select the "Prospective" and "History" checkboxes
Under Existing Clients/Policies, check both the Prospective and History checkboxes. This expands the search scope to include policies across all lifecycle stages — ensuring expired or non-renewed policies appear alongside active ones.
Why both? Download files may arrive after a policy has lapsed or been renewed. Checking History prevents the correct policy from being excluded from search results simply because it is no longer active.
3
Click "Find" to refresh the policy search results
Click Find to execute the updated search with the expanded scope. The results list will refresh to include all matching policies — prospective, active, and historical.
Phase 02 Policy Selection and Assignment
4
Click the Expiration column header to sort the policies
Click the Expiration column header to sort the results by expiration date. This groups policies chronologically, making it much faster to locate the policy whose effective date range aligns with the downloaded file.
5
Find the policy with the matching effective date range
Scan the sorted list for the policy whose effective and expiration dates correspond to the period covered by the downloaded file. The dates on the download transaction should align with the policy term shown in the results.
Date matching is critical: Assigning a download to the wrong policy term creates incorrect coverage history and can cause billing discrepancies. Confirm the effective date on the download matches the policy term before proceeding.
6
Click "Select Existing"
With the correct policy highlighted, click Select Existing. This tells Epic you are assigning the suspense item to an existing policy record rather than creating a new one.
7
Click "Update Existing" in the Suspense Policy Line Detail window
The Suspense Policy Line Detail window will appear. Click Update Existing to confirm that the downloaded transaction should be merged into the selected policy record.
8
Click "Yes" to confirm the policy update
A confirmation prompt will appear asking you to verify the policy update. Click Yes to proceed. Epic will write the downloaded data to the selected policy record.
No undo: Once confirmed, the assignment is applied to the policy record. Double-check the policy term in step 5 before clicking Yes.
9
Verify the Lookup Code is assigned to the policy
After confirming, verify that the Lookup Code has been assigned to the policy. This confirms the download has been successfully linked — the suspense item should no longer appear in the queue for this transaction.
Workflow complete. If the Lookup Code does not appear, the assignment may not have saved correctly. Refresh the suspense queue and attempt the assignment again from step 1.
Common Questions
Why do I need to check both Prospective and History? ▾

By default, the policy search in the suspense assignment screen may only return active policies. Carrier downloads can arrive days or weeks after a policy term has ended — particularly for endorsements, audits, or final premium adjustments. Checking both Prospective and History ensures the full policy timeline is visible, so you can assign the download to the correct term even if that policy is no longer current.

What if no policy matches the effective date on the download? ▾

If no existing policy term aligns with the dates on the downloaded file, do not force an assignment to the closest match. Instead, check whether the policy was set up in Epic under a different account, or whether it was entered with incorrect dates. In some cases the policy genuinely does not exist in Epic yet and will need to be created before the download can be assigned. Contact your Epic administrator if you cannot locate the correct policy after checking History and Prospective.

What does the Lookup Code confirm? ▾

The Lookup Code is the carrier's identifier that links the downloaded transaction to a specific policy record in Epic. When it appears on the policy after assignment, it confirms that the IVANS download has been successfully attached and that future downloads from the same carrier for that policy will route automatically — bypassing the suspense queue. If the Lookup Code is missing after assignment, the link was not established and the next download will return to suspense.

What is the difference between "Select Existing" and creating a new policy? ▾

Select Existing attaches the downloaded file to a policy record that already exists in Epic. Creating a new policy would generate a new record from the download data — appropriate only when the policy is not yet in the system at all. In most suspense scenarios the policy already exists and Select Existing is the correct choice. Creating a duplicate policy record causes significant problems with billing, commission, and document history, so only use the new policy option when you have confirmed the policy does not exist anywhere in Epic.
