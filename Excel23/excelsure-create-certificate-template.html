Overview

This workflow starts in Applied Epic and moves into CSR24 to build a new Certificate of Liability Insurance template. You will navigate through the client file in Epic, launch the account in CSR24, configure the template header, attach the GL policy, set coverage selections, select a signer, then upload and link any endorsement attachments.

Phase 01 Navigate from Epic to CSR24
1
Starting in Epic, click "Proofs of Insurance"
Open the client's file in Applied Epic and click Proofs of Insurance in the navigation.
2
Click "Premium Processing"
Within Proofs of Insurance, click Premium Processing.
3
Click "Certificates" to open the account in CSR24
Click Certificates. This will redirect you out of Epic and into the client's account in CSR24.
4
In CSR24, click "Proofs"
Once inside CSR24, click Proofs in the navigation menu.
5
Click "Certificate Templates"
Under Proofs, click Certificate Templates to open the template management screen for this account.
6
Click the Add (+) button to create a new template
Click the Add (+) button to open the new template form.
Phase 02 Configure the New Template
7
Enter the Template Origination and Expiration dates
Fill in both date fields:
  • 1Template Origination Date — today's date.
  • 2Template Expiration Date — the closest expiration date of any policy that will be listed on the certificate.
Why the expiration date matters: The certificate becomes invalid once any listed policy passes its expiration date. By setting the Template Expiration to the closest policy expiration, the template will turn red after that date — giving you a visual indicator that the template needs updating before new certificates can be issued.
8
Enter the "Description for Agency's Use"
This is an internal description visible only to agency employees — certificate holders never see it. Use a naming convention that identifies the policy year and template type. Example: 25/26 Master Template
9
Enter the "Description to be Displayed"
This is the template name shown to clients in the self-service portal when they are issuing their own certificates. Most users copy the Agency's Use description and paste it into this field.
10
Select "ACORD 25 (2016/03) Certificate of Liability Insurance"
From the form type dropdown, select ACORD 25 (2016/03) Certificate of Liability Insurance.
11
Click "Add"
Click Add to commit the form type selection and open the full certificate template layout.
12
The certificate layout and additional fields are now available
After clicking Add, the page expands to display the full ACORD 25 certificate layout with all configurable fields. You will complete this form in the steps below.
13
Check "Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status"
Select the Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status checkbox. This allows Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation status to be controlled at the individual holder level — so the master template stays flexible and holders get the correct AI and WVD designations based on their contract requirements.
14
Look up the Producer contact and click "Lookup"
In the Producer area of the certificate, look up the contact name at the agency you want listed on the certificate. Click Lookup to open the contact search.
15
Find the name in the library and select it
Search the contact library, locate the correct agency contact name, and select it. The producer details will populate on the certificate template.
Phase 03 Attach the Policy & Set Coverage Selections
16
Under General Liability, select "Add Policy"
In the General Liability section of the certificate layout, click Add Policy to open the policy selection workflow.
17
Use the dropdown to select the correct liability policy
A workflow box will open. Use the dropdown to select the appropriate liability policy from the client's policies in Epic.
18
Click "Save"
Click Save to attach the selected policy to the certificate template.
19
Verify that policy details have populated
After saving, the limits of insurance, policy number, effective and expiration dates, and other ACORD fields will auto-populate from the Epic policy data.
ACORD data accuracy is critical: CSR24 only pulls what is recorded on the ACORD in Epic. If expected fields are blank or missing, you have two options:

Option A — Update in CSR24 directly: Manually correct the template in CSR24. Changes are instant and become part of this template, but Epic ACORDs are not updated.

Option B — Update in Epic and sync: Go back into Epic, endorse the policy, make ACORD corrections, issue the endorsement, and wait for the sync to CSR24. The sync can take up to one hour — check back periodically. This is the preferred approach as it keeps Epic and CSR24 aligned.
20
Pull up a prior certificate as a reference (if available)
If you have a recent certificate issued from the incumbent system, pull it up alongside CSR24 as a visual reference while building the template. This helps confirm the correct coverage selections are being made.
21
Verify the coverage selections and correct any missing fields
Review the populated ACORD fields against your reference. If selections are missing, you can add them directly on the template (Option A) or update Epic and wait for the sync (Option B). Note: Epic-to-CSR24 sync delay is up to one hour.
22
Make the appropriate coverage selections
Work through each coverage section and confirm all required checkboxes and selections are correctly set to match the prior certificate and the client's policy.
23
Set Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation to "Yes" on all applicable lines
On every coverage line where AI or Waiver of Subrogation may be required, set both fields to Yes. Because "Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status" is checked, these settings will be finalized and controlled at the individual holder level — the template-level "Yes" just ensures the options are available.
24
Complete all coverage sections required for this template
Finish configuring all coverage lines. When complete, the template should reflect all the same selections as the reference certificate from Epic.
25
Scroll to the bottom and select the Signature Option
At the bottom of the certificate template, in the Signature Option field, select the name of the person whose name and signature will appear on issued certificates.
26
Scroll to the top and click "Save"
Once all coverages are listed and the signer is selected, scroll back to the top of the page and click Save to save the completed template.
27
Confirm the template is saved and available
After saving, you will see the new template listed in the Certificate Templates view. It is now available to add holders to.
Template complete. If this account has no endorsement attachments that need to be included with certificates, the workflow ends here. If endorsement documents need to be attached to the template, continue to Phase 4.
Phase 04 Add Certificate Attachments to the Template
28
Click "Cert Attachments"
Navigate to the Cert Attachments section to begin uploading the endorsement documents that will be attached to certificates when issued.
29
Click the Add (+) button
Click Add (+) to open the attachment upload dialog.
30
Choose your upload method
There are two ways to get endorsement documents into CSR24:
  • ADirect Upload from Local Computer — upload a file saved on your machine.
  • BEpic Attachment Add — click Search, navigate to the Certificate Endorsement folder in Epic, and select the document directly from Epic's attachment library.
31
Click "Choose File" and locate the file
If uploading from your local computer, click Choose File, navigate to the endorsement document, and select it. If using Epic Attachment Add, use the search to navigate the Epic folder structure and select the attachment from there.
32
Enter the Display Name of the attached endorsement
Enter a clear, descriptive display name for the endorsement document. This name will be visible on the certificate attachment list.
33
Click "Import" to upload the attachment into the client file
Click Import to bring the attachment into the client's file within CSR24.
34
You will be directed to the Import Details page
CSR24 will navigate you to the Import Details page confirming the attachment has been uploaded to the client file.
35
Navigate back to "Certificate Templates"
Return to the Certificate Templates view to link the uploaded attachment to your template.
36
Locate the template and click the hyperlink under the "Attach" column
Find your newly created template in the list. Click the hyperlink in the Attach column to open the attachment linking screen for that template.
37
Select the appropriate attachment(s)
Check the box next to each endorsement document that should be included with certificates issued from this template.
38
Set the order number if multiple attachments exist
If there are multiple endorsement attachments, use the order number field (1, 2, 3, etc.) to control the sequence in which attachments appear when certificates are issued.
39
Click "Save"
Click Save to link the selected attachments to the template.
40
Confirm the attachment count on the template
You will now see the attachment count updated on the template row — for example, 1 Attachment. This confirms the endorsement is linked and will be included when certificates are issued from this template.
Workflow complete. The certificate template is fully configured with policy data, coverage selections, a signer, and any required endorsement attachments. It is ready for holders to be added.
Common Questions
Why did my coverage fields not populate after attaching the policy?▼

CSR24 only pulls data that is recorded on the ACORD in Epic. If fields are blank or missing after attaching the policy in step 18–19, the selections are not set on the Epic ACORD. You can either correct them directly on the CSR24 template (instant, Option A) or update the Epic ACORD, issue the endorsement, and wait up to one hour for the sync to bring the data into CSR24 (Option B, preferred).

What does "Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status" actually do?▼

Checking this box in step 13 allows Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation to be toggled on or off at the individual holder level, rather than being locked at the template level. This means one template can serve holders who require AI and WVD as well as holders who do not — you set the per-holder status when adding or managing holders, not by building separate templates.

What is the Template Expiration Date used for?▼

The Template Expiration Date is set to the closest policy expiration date among all policies listed on the certificate. Once that date passes, the template turns red in the Certificate Templates list — a visual signal that one or more underlying policies have expired and the template must be updated before it can be used to issue valid certificates. It does not prevent certificate issuance automatically, but it is your warning flag to review and renew the template.

Can I upload endorsement attachments from Epic instead of my local computer?▼

Yes. In steps 30–31, when you click Add (+) to upload an attachment, you will see an "Epic Attachment Add" option below the local file upload field. Clicking that opens a search interface that mirrors Epic's folder structure — navigate to the Certificate Endorsement folder, locate the document, and select it directly from Epic without needing to save it locally first.
