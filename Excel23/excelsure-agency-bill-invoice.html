Invoicing: Create an Agency Bill Invoice

Overview

This workflow covers how to create an Agency Bill invoice in Applied Epic from a carrier invoice received via email. You will receive and review the carrier invoice, enter the premium and all additional transaction lines (fees, taxes), verify totals, set the delivery method, confirm commissions, generate the final invoice, and attach the carrier invoice to the resulting INVC activity. The workflow covers 4 phases: receiving the carrier invoice, starting the invoice in Epic, generating the final invoice and INVC activity, and attaching the carrier invoice to that activity.


Phase 1 — Receive the Carrier Invoice (Steps 1–6): The carrier invoice arrives via email. Open and review the invoice, navigate to Epic with the invoice visible on a second screen, highlight the correct policy, click New, and select Transaction.


Phase 2 — Start the Invoice (Steps 7–27): Confirm Policy is selected, select the policy, add the first transaction line using the appropriate premium code (RENB for renewal, NEWB for new business, ENDT for endorsement, CANC for cancellation), enter the premium amount, and click Continue. Add each additional line item from the carrier invoice — fees, taxes, and any agency fee — using the correct transaction codes (CFEE for company fees, AFEE for agency fees). Update the description on each line to match the carrier invoice, enter the correct charge, and click Add. When all lines are entered, click Finish. Verify that the Epic total matches the carrier invoice total, then click Invoice. Set the Delivery Method to Mail, click Commissions, verify the line commission percentage, and click Finish.


Phase 3 — Generate the Final Invoice & INVC Activity (Steps 28–39): After finishing the invoice entry you are redirected to the Transactions page. Click Actions, then Generate Invoice to open the Generate Invoice workflow. Click Distribution, verify it is set to Print, and click Finish. The system creates an INVC activity. Update the Activity Description line, verify the follow-up date (typically 15 days before the invoice due date), enter a follow-up note, and click Finish. A copy of the invoice will display — close it, as it automatically attaches to the INVC activity.


Phase 4 — Attach Carrier Invoice to INVC Activity (Steps 40–46): Click Activities and highlight the INVC activity just created. Return to the email (or desktop) containing the carrier invoice and drag and drop it directly onto the INVC activity. Click Finish. Spot check the activity attachments to confirm both the Epic-generated invoice and the carrier invoice are present by clicking Access, then Attachments, and verifying both documents appear.

Common Questions
Which transaction code should I use for a renewal invoice?▼

Use RENB (Renewal Business) for renewal invoices. The four main premium transaction codes are RENB for renewals, NEWB for new business, ENDT for endorsements, and CANC for cancellations. Always match the transaction code to the type of activity the invoice represents — using the wrong code can cause reporting and workflow issues downstream.

The Epic total doesn't match the carrier invoice — what do I do?▼

First, check whether an Agency Fee (AFEE) was added to the Epic invoice. If so, the Epic total will intentionally be higher than the carrier invoice by the amount of the agency fee — this is expected. If no agency fee was added and the totals still don't match, review each line item in the Epic invoice and compare it against the carrier invoice line by line. Look for a missed line, a data entry error, or a line that was entered with the wrong amount. Correct the discrepancy before clicking Invoice.

What is the AFEE transaction code used for?▼

AFEE stands for Agency Fee. It is used when the agency wants to collect an additional fee on top of the carrier's invoice for placing the policy. When an AFEE is included, the Epic invoice total will be higher than the carrier invoice total by the amount of the agency fee. This is intentional and correct — the agency fee is revenue collected directly by the agency, not from the carrier.

What is the follow-up date on the INVC activity for?▼

The follow-up date on the INVC activity is a reminder to review and clear the accounts receivable (A/R) balance before the invoice is due. The standard convention is to set it 15 days before the invoice due date. This gives the agency enough lead time to follow up with the insured if payment has not been received, rather than discovering an outstanding balance after the due date has passed.

Do I need to save the invoice PDF that opens after clicking Finish?▼

No. The invoice PDF that appears after generating the invoice is automatically attached to the INVC activity. You can close that window without saving it — it is already on the activity. The separate step of dragging the carrier invoice onto the INVC activity is for the carrier's document, not the Epic-generated invoice.
