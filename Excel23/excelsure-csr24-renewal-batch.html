Process a Renewal Batch
for Commercial Insurance

Step-by-step walkthrough for generating and managing commercial insurance certificate renewals in CSR24 — covering holder reconciliation, copying the expiring template, linking renewed policies, updating endorsement attachments, and issuing the batch.

43 Steps

Certificate Renewal

Before You Start

Before beginning the renewal batch, open the updated certificate holder list attached to the CERR activity in Epic. You will need this list to reconcile which holders should be included in the upcoming renewal — comparing it against the current holder list in CSR24 before starting the process. Also confirm that all renewed policies have synced from Applied Epic to CSR24 before proceeding.

Start Renewal ↓ Update Holders ↓ Update Template ↓ Attachments & Renew ↓
Phase 01 Start Renewal Process
1
Start on the client account in CSR24
Open the insured's account in CSR24. Confirm you are on the correct account before proceeding — all renewal activity is account-specific.
Before you begin: Open the updated certificate holder list attached to the CERR activity in Epic. You will need to compare this list against the current holders in CSR24 during the next phase.
2
Click "Proofs"
From the account navigation, click Proofs to access the certificate and renewal processing area.
3
Click "Renewals / Batch"
Under Proofs, click Renewals / Batch to open the renewal processing screen for this account.
4
Click "Start New Renewal"
Click Start New Renewal to begin the renewal batch process for this account.
Phase 02 Update Holders & Start Renewal Process
5
Reconcile the holder list against the updated CERR list from Epic
Using the updated certificate holder list from the CERR activity in Epic, review each holder currently shown in CSR24. To remove a holder from the upcoming renewal batch, uncheck the box next to their name. Only holders that should receive a renewal certificate should remain checked.
6
Click "Start Process"
Once the holder list is reconciled, click Start Process to initiate the renewal batch.
Phase 03 Copy Expiring Certificate & Update Template
7
Click "Edit"
Click the Edit button to begin editing the expiring certificate template for the renewal.
8
Select "Edit a Copy of This Template"
Select the radio button that says Edit a Copy of This Template. This preserves the original expiring template — allowing prior-year certificates to still be accessed — while creating a new copy to update with the renewed policy terms.
9
Enter the new Policy Year Description
Update the template description to reflect the new policy year. Use a clear naming convention that identifies the policy term (e.g. 26/27 Master w/ AI endt).
10
Click "Edit Template"
Click Edit Template to open the copied template and begin updating it with the renewed policy information.
11
Check "Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status"
Confirm the Enable holder level AI & Subr WVD Status checkbox is checked on the copied template. This ensures Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation remain controllable at the individual holder level.
12
Select the current (expired) policy on the first line of business
Starting with the first line of business on the template, click on the currently attached policy to open its details. This will be the prior-year policy that has expired and needs to be replaced.
13
Click "Delete" to remove the expired policy term
The expired policy details will appear. Click Delete to remove the prior-year policy from this coverage line so the renewed policy can be attached in its place.
14
Click "Add Policy"
Click Add Policy to open the policy selection dropdown for this coverage line.
15
Select the renewed policy for this line of business
Locate the renewed policy with the new term in the dropdown and select it. The new policy details — policy number, effective date, expiration date, and limits — will fill into the form from the Epic sync.
Policy not showing? If the renewed policy does not appear in the list, it has not yet synced from Applied Epic to CSR24. The sync can take up to one hour. Do not manually add a policy — wait for the sync and return to this step.
16
Click "Save"
Click Save to attach the renewed policy to this coverage line on the template.
17
Check Type of Insurance box and set AI / Waiver of Subrogation
Check the Type of Insurance checkbox for this coverage line. If the policy includes Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation, select Y for both fields.
18
Verify that coverages pulled in correctly
Review the coverage limits and fields populated from the renewed policy to confirm they are accurate. If any fields appear incorrect, the issue is in the Epic policy record — correct it there and wait for the sync rather than manually overriding in CSR24.
19
Repeat for all remaining policies on the template, then click "Save"
Work through each remaining coverage line on the copied template — delete the expired policy, add the renewed policy, set AI/WVD, verify coverages. When all lines are updated, click Save.
20
Update the Certificate Signature if needed
If the signing authority has changed since the prior year, update the Certificate Signature field to reflect the correct authorized signer for the new policy term.
21
Click "Save"
Click Save to commit all template updates before moving to the endorsement attachments section.
Phase 04 Assign Endorsement Documents & Renew the Certificates
22
Click "Attachments"
Click Attachments to open the endorsement document attachment screen for the updated template.
23
Check for endorsements in the available documents area
Review the available documents list to confirm the required endorsement documents are present. If the endorsements are already available, skip ahead to step 35 to select and attach them.
If endorsements are not in the list — they need to be imported from Epic before they can be attached. Continue through steps 24–34 to import them.
24
Click "Save" and navigate to "Cert Attachments"
Click Save, then navigate to Cert Attachments to begin importing endorsement documents from Epic.
25
Click the Add (+) icon
Click the Add (+) icon to open the attachment import dialog.
26
Click "Click Section To Load Epic Attachments"
Click Click Section To Load Epic Attachments to connect to the Epic attachment library for this account.
27
Locate the folder "1. Certificates: #END Documents"
In the Epic attachment library, navigate to the folder called 1. Certificates: #END Documents. This is where endorsement documents are stored for certificate use.
28
Locate and highlight the file to import
Find the endorsement document you need to attach and click to highlight it.
29
Click "Import"
Click the Import button to bring the endorsement document into the CSR24 attachment library for this account.
30
Review the results page — repeat for any additional documents
The results page will confirm the import. If additional endorsement documents need to be imported, return to Cert Templates and repeat steps 25–29 for each document until all required endorsements are in the available documents list.
31
When all attachments are imported, click "Renewals / Batch"
Once all endorsement documents are in the library, click Renewals / Batch to return to the renewal processing screen.
32
Click "Continue / View Current Renewals"
Click Continue / View Current Renewals to return to the Renewal Cert Processing page where the updated template is waiting.
33
Click "Attachments" on the Renewal Cert Processing page
Back on the Renewal Cert Processing page, click Attachments to open the endorsement selection list for the renewed template.
34
Select the attachments to include and click "Save"
Check the box next to each endorsement document that should be included with the renewed certificates. Click Save to link the selected attachments to the template.
35
Click "Preview"
Click Preview to generate a PDF preview of the certificate and all attached endorsement documents. Review carefully for accuracy — holder information, coverage limits, policy dates, AI/WVD selections, Description of Operations, and attachments — before proceeding to issue.
Make corrections now. Any errors caught in preview can be fixed before issuing. Once you click Renew, certificates are distributed to all holders with email addresses on file.
36
After approving the preview, click "Renew"
Once the preview looks correct, click Renew to begin the batch renewal process.
37
Set "Send Certificate with no Recipient Info" to No Destination
For holders who have no email address on file, set the delivery option to No Destination. A ZIP file containing all certificates will be downloaded after the batch completes — use this to manually distribute to any holders without email addresses.
38
Check "Start sending after Renewal Completes"
Check the Start sending after Renewal Completes checkbox. This ensures that all holders with a recipient email address on file will automatically receive their certificate by email once the renewal batch finishes processing.
How delivery works: Holders with a recipient email receive the certificate by email automatically. Holders without an email address are included in the downloaded ZIP file for manual distribution to the insured.
39
Click "Renew"
Click Renew to execute the batch. CSR24 will process and issue certificates to all holders in the renewal list.
40
Review the status page with results
Once the batch is complete, a status page will display showing the results for all holders — how many were processed, how many were emailed, and any that encountered an error. Review this page and address any failures before closing out the renewal.
Workflow complete. To reprint certificates or send the ZIP file to the insured for manual distribution, use the Reprint Certificates option under Manage Certificates.
Common Questions
Why should I always select "Edit a Copy of This Template" rather than editing the original?▾

Editing a copy preserves the original expiring template exactly as it was, which means prior-year certificates can still be accessed and reprinted from the old template if a holder requests one. If you edited the original directly, those prior certificates would reference updated policy information that didn't exist when they were issued. Creating a copy for each renewal year also gives you a clean audit trail — one template per policy term.

The renewed policy isn't showing in the Add Policy dropdown — what do I do?▾

The policy hasn't synced from Applied Epic to CSR24 yet. The sync can take up to one hour after the policy is entered or updated in Epic. Do not manually add or type in the policy — wait for the sync and then return to step 15. Manually added policies are not reflected anywhere in Epic and can cause coverage validation issues later.

What happens to holders without an email address in the renewal batch?▾

Holders without a recipient email address on file do not receive an email when the batch runs. Their certificates are included in the ZIP file that downloads after the renewal completes. You then forward this ZIP (or the individual PDFs inside it) to the insured, who is responsible for distributing them to the relevant holders. This is also why setting "Send Certificate with no Recipient Info" to No Destination is important — it prevents the system from attempting delivery to a blank address.

Should I turn off "Send a copy to insured" before running the batch?▾

Yes — this is one of the most important pre-batch checks. If the account's "Send a copy of all certificates to this insured" setting is turned on, the insured will receive one email for every single holder certificate issued during the batch. On a large account with 200+ holders, that's 200+ emails hitting the insured's inbox simultaneously. Turn this setting to No before running the batch, and restore it to By Email when daily certificate processing resumes afterward.

Where do I find issued certificates after the batch completes?▾

All issued certificates from the renewal batch are accessible under Reprint Certificates in the Manage Certificates menu. You can search by holder name, date, or certificate number to locate individual certificates. The batch results page also provides a summary immediately after processing completes — note any holders that show errors and reissue individually as needed.
