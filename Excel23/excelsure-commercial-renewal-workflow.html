Phase 02 Select Your Billing Path
Billing Type
Direct Bill — What happens next

The RENR activity is assigned to the Account Manager to monitor. The agency waits for the renewal policy or download from the carrier, set for 45 days prior to expiration.

How is the policy received?
Carrier download received: The >REN activity is auto-assigned. The download automatically updates all ACORDs, estimated premiums, effective dates, and policy number.
1
Verify policy details Account Manager
Open the >REN activity and verify/update: servicing role designations, line commission percentage, and PR/BR commissionable producer sections of the policy line details.
2
Secure policy documents Account Manager
Check activities for a ^POL activity code — two scenarios apply:
Scenario A — ^POL Activity Exists
A ^POL has been received — the PDF is already attached to the policy in Epic and to the ^POL activity. Close the ^POL activity.
Scenario B — No ^POL Activity
The >REN download indicates documents are available on the carrier website. Complete the following:
  1. 1. Log into the carrier website and find the renewal document.
  2. 2. Download the policy document.
  3. 3. Drag and drop the policy copy to the Active Policy Line in Epic.
  4. 4. Create a #POL activity code and attach the document.
  5. 5. Close the #POL activity.
Note: For Direct Bill policies, the carrier delivers copies of the policy directly to the insured. No policy delivery email is required from the agency.
3
Update Renewal Manager Account Manager
Update the Renewal Manager stage to the final completed stage to remove the policy from the Renewal Manager list.
4
Close >REN activity Account Manager
Close the >REN activity with a note stating the Renewal Manager stage has been updated.
5
Close RENR activity Account Manager
Close the original RENR activity.
If the download does not arrive by 45 days prior to expiration, the Account Manager should contact the Department Manager for direction on next steps.

Agency Bill — What happens next

Per the Department Manager's direction from Phase 01, the RENR activity has been reassigned to either the Account Manager (wait for incumbent pricing) or the Marketing Team (remarket immediately).

What is the next action?
The Account Manager owns the RENR activity and sets it for a future date — the point at which they want to take action if the policy has not yet been delivered to the agency.

Phase 03 Remarketing Process
Applies to both billing types

For Direct Bill policies, if the renewal is received and a decision is made to remarket, follow the same steps below. The process is identical regardless of billing type.

1
Create master application Responsible Party
Create the Master Application in Epic. This generates an MPOL activity to track the submission process. Once the MPOL is created, close the RENR activity.
2
Update master application Responsible Party
Update all ACORD data for the future renewal term across all lines being remarketed. Attach all supporting documents: loss runs, supplemental applications, and any other required underwriting data. The Master Application is the central repository for all data.
3
Create carrier submissions Responsible Party
Use the Actions button to create carrier submissions. Each submission replicates the Master Application for the selected carriers. Include or remove attachments as applicable per carrier.
4
Submit to carriers Responsible Party
Two options:
  • Submit directly to underwriting: Actions → Submit to Carrier → generates SCAC activity
  • Quoted online: Actions → Update Stage to Submitted/Entered → generates SCAO activity
SCA activities track each individual submission and all underwriting correspondence until quotes are received.
Phase 04 Carrier Response — Quote or Declination
1
Create carrier response Responsible Party
As soon as a response is received, use Actions → Create Carrier Response to update the carrier's response, issuing paper, and all quote details.
2
Attach documentation Responsible Party
Drag and drop the quote and accompanying documents to the carrier submission.
3
Create #QUO activity Responsible Party
  • Quote received: Create #QUO activity normally.
  • Declination received: Create #QUO with "Declined" added at the end of the description.
Assign to the responsible party for review. Close the SCA activity.
Repeat this process for each carrier submission sent out.
If you update the carrier response applications with the returned quote details and that carrier becomes the winning carrier, no further ACORD updates will be needed when the policy moves to the active screen.
Phase 05 Proposal Stage
1
Send proposal Responsible Party
Create and send the proposal to the client with one or several quotes for review. From Outlook sent items, drag and drop the sent email with the proposal to the carrier submission.
2
Create #PRO activity Responsible Party
The #PRO activity tracks all correspondence between the agency and the client regarding the proposal — documents, questions, and emails all logged here.
3
Close prior activities Responsible Party
Once #PRO is created and the proposal has been sent, close all #QUO activities and the MPOL activity. All tracking from this point is through #PRO.
4
Client confirms — create #BIN Responsible Party
Upon receipt of client confirmation to move forward: drag and drop the confirmation email to the carrier submission of the winning market. Create #BIN activity and close #PRO.
Phase 06 Binding
Required Documentation

The #BIN activity is the complete record of all binding correspondence and must contain three documents: the client's binding request, the agency's request to the carrier, and the carrier's confirmation.

1
Client binding request
The original request from the insured — attached when the #BIN is created.
2
Agency request to carrier Responsible Party
Documentation of the binding request sent to the carrier — either by sending directly to the carrier or by logging into the carrier portal to bind. Documented within the #BIN activity.
3
Carrier confirmation
When the carrier returns the binder, it is logged and attached to the #BIN activity. The #BIN should now contain all three documents as a complete audit trail.
Phase 07 Move to Active Screen & Policy Issuance
1
Complete binding in Epic Responsible Party
Return to the winning carrier submission. Use the Actions menu → Move to Active Screen.
2
Set policy structure Responsible Party
The workflow moves the winning lines from Master Marketing to the active policy screen in Epic. This is the opportunity to structure the policy — move lines as a package or as mono lines.
3
Verify data and issue Responsible Party
Verify all data on the active screen, then issue the policies.
Common Questions
What is the difference between Direct Bill and Agency Bill in this workflow?▼

For both billing types, the RENR activity is created at 120 days and routed to the Department Manager at 127 days for a marketing decision. Once the Department Manager updates the RENR notes and reassigns the activity, the path diverges: for Direct Bill, the account manager monitors for the carrier download; for Agency Bill, the account manager or marketing team takes action based on the Department Manager's direction.

What happens during the 127–120 day window?▼

This is the Department Manager review window. The Department Manager reviews the policy, determines whether to wait for incumbent pricing or remarket, updates the RENR activity notes with that decision, and reassigns the activity to the appropriate party — the responsible account manager or the marketing team.

Can a Direct Bill policy be remarketed?▼

Yes. If the account manager receives the renewal on a Direct Bill account and the Department Manager determines the pricing is not competitive, the policy can be remarketed. The remarketing process is identical for both billing types — follow the same steps from Phase 03 onward.

What happens if the carrier download never arrives on a Direct Bill policy?▼

If the download does not arrive by 45 days prior to expiration, the account manager who holds the RENR activity should contact the Department Manager for direction on next steps.

Why does updating the carrier response applications matter?▼

When you update the carrier response applications with the quote details returned by the carrier, that data carries forward if that carrier becomes the winning market. When you use Actions → Move to Active Screen, the ACORD data is already current — no additional updates are needed at issuance.

What three documents must be in the #BIN activity before binding is complete?▼

The #BIN activity must contain: (1) the original binding request from the insured, (2) documentation of the agency's request sent to the carrier or a record of logging into the carrier portal to bind, and (3) the carrier's binder confirmation once returned. These three documents form the complete audit trail for the binding transaction.
