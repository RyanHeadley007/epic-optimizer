Exporting Issued Certs
into a Zip Folder

Access and download master general liability and workers compensation certificates through the CSR24 portal. This guide covers generating zip files with all required attachments for your records — from the account level through to the downloaded file.

What This Guide Covers

Use this workflow whenever you need to bulk-export issued certificates for a client — typically as part of a certificate renewal or delivery cycle. The Zip Certs function in CSR24 packages all holder certificates and their attachments into a single downloadable zip file. Once downloaded, send the certificates to the insured, attach the sent item to the open CERR activity, update notes that certificates have been delivered, and close the CERR.

Navigate to Zip Certs ↓ Configure & Export ↓
Phase 01 Navigate to Zip Certs
1
Start at the account level in CSR24
Open CSR24 and navigate to the client account you need to export certificates for. All steps in this workflow begin from the account-level view.
2
Click "Proofs"
From the account view, click the Proofs tab. This opens the certificate and proof of insurance section for the account, where issued certificates and holder lists are managed.
3
Click "Zip Certs"
Inside the Proofs section, click Zip Certs. This opens the bulk certificate export tool, which will package all issued certificates for the selected holder template into a single zip file.
4
Select the Holder Template to export from
Choose the Holder Template you want to export the holder list from. Templates typically correspond to a specific coverage type or certificate form — for example, a master GL template or a workers compensation template.
Multiple active templates: If more than one template is marked as active, you must repeat this download process for each active template separately. Exporting from only one will leave certificates from the other template out of your records.
Phase 02 Configure and Export
5
Click "Download"
Click the Download button to open the export configuration options. This is where you will set your attachment preference before generating the zip file.
6
Check the "Include Attachments?" field
Click the Include Attachments? checkbox to mark it selected. This ensures all supporting documents — such as endorsements or additional insured forms attached to the certificates — are included in the zip file alongside the certificate PDFs.
7
Click "Submit"
Click Submit to begin generating the zip file. CSR24 will process the request and prepare the certificates and attachments for download.
8
Click "Click here"
After submitting, a confirmation message will appear with a Click here link. Click it to proceed to the final download confirmation screen.
9
Click "Submit" again to confirm
Click Submit a second time to confirm and trigger the zip file download. This final submission tells CSR24 to deliver the packaged file to your browser.
10
Zip file downloads to your Downloads folder
The zip file will automatically download to your computer's Downloads folder. The file contains all issued certificates for the selected holder template, along with any attachments you selected to include.
11
Click "Close" to finish
Click Close to dismiss the export dialog and return to the account view. The workflow is complete.
Next steps: Send the certificates to the insured, attach the sent item to the open CERR activity, update the notes to confirm certificates have been delivered, and close the CERR activity.
Common Questions
What is a Holder Template and why does it matter? ▾

A Holder Template defines the structure and coverage details used to generate certificates for a specific group of certificate holders. For example, a client may have one template for general liability certificates and another for workers compensation. Each template produces its own set of certificates, so exporting from the correct template ensures holders receive the right coverage information. If multiple templates are active, all of them need to be exported to capture the full set of issued certificates.

Why should I check "Include Attachments?" ▾

Many certificates reference or require supporting documents — additional insured endorsements, waivers of subrogation, or other policy forms. These attachments are stored alongside the certificate in CSR24 and are often required by the certificate holder as part of proof of coverage. Checking Include Attachments ensures the zip file is complete and ready to send without having to locate and attach those documents manually after the fact.

What should I do if more than one template is active? ▾

If an account has multiple active holder templates — such as separate templates for GL and WC — you need to run the Zip Certs export once for each active template. Each run produces its own zip file. After downloading both, you will have the complete set of certificates and attachments for all coverage types. Exporting only one template will result in missing certificates for the holders covered under the other template.

What are the next steps after downloading the zip file? ▾

Once the zip file is in your Downloads folder: (1) Send the certificates to the insured via email or the appropriate delivery method. (2) Attach the sent email or confirmation to the open CERR activity in Epic. (3) Update the activity notes to document that certificates have been delivered. (4) Close the CERR activity to complete the certificate issuance workflow. Keeping the CERR updated ensures a clear audit trail and prevents the activity from showing as outstanding in follow-up queues.
