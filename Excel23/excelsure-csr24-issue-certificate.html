How to Issue a
Certificate of Insurance in CSR24

Step-by-step walkthrough for issuing a Certificate of Insurance through CSR24 — from selecting the certificate template and holder through completing the Description of Operations, previewing, and submitting.

4 Phases
40 Steps
Applied Epic · CSR24
Certificate Issuance

Overview

Follow this guide to navigate Applied Epic and generate a certificate of insurance for a commercial account. This workflow covers accessing CSR24 from Epic, selecting the correct template, adding holder information, setting Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation status, completing the Description of Operations, configuring delivery, and verifying the certificate back in Epic.

Access CSR24 ↓ Issue Certificate ↓ Select / Add Holder ↓ Delivery & Submit ↓
Phase 01 Access CSR24 to Issue Certificate
1
Start on the "Client" home screen of Epic
Open the insured's account in Applied Epic and confirm you are on the Client home screen for the correct account before proceeding.
2
Click "Proofs of Insurance" → "Premium Processing" → "Certificates"
From the Epic navigation, click Proofs of Insurance, then Premium Processing, then Certificates. This launches CSR24 for the account.
3
You will be directed to CSR24 and land on the client home screen
CSR24 opens to the client's account home screen. All certificate issuance for this insured is done from here.
Phase 02 Issue a New Certificate
4
Click "Issue Cert"
Click the Issue Cert button on the client home screen in CSR24 to begin the certificate issuance process.
5
Review the list of available templates
You will be redirected to the Issue a Certificate screen showing all available templates for this account.
If a template is RED, do not issue. A red template means it has expired — typically because a policy is outside its effective date range. Do not issue a certificate from a red template. See your direct support contact for next steps.
6
Select the correct template based on the holder's requirements
Based on what the holder is requesting, select the appropriate template. For example, if the holder needs to be listed as an Additional Insured for General Liability, select the 25/26 Master w/ AI endt template.
Template selection drives attachments. Endorsement documents are assigned at the template level. If you select the AI template, a copy of the AI endorsement will automatically be attached to the certificate upon issuance.
7
Verify limits on the template view
After selecting a template you will be taken to the template view where you can verify coverage limits and confirm it is the correct template. Do not worry about the Additional Insured or Waiver of Subrogation selections here — you will set those per holder in the next steps.
Phase 03 Select / Add Holder
8
Click "Select Holder" to search before adding
Even if you believe the holder is new, always start by clicking Select Holder to search the existing list and confirm you are not creating a duplicate. You can add a new holder from the next screen if they are not found.
9
Search for the holder — if not found, click "Add Holder"
Search the holder list. If the holder is not present, click the Add Holder button to add them as a new holder on this account.
10
Enter the holder name and address
Complete all required fields — holder name and full mailing address. The name and address must match exactly what the holder's contract specifies.
11
Consider the "One Time Only" selection
If the certificate holder only needs to be named this single time and will not require a renewal certificate, select One Time Only. This is typically used for special events and short-term policies. If selecting One Time Only, set the Date Interest Ends to the day after the contract ends.
12
Check "Do they Receive Renewals?" if applicable
If the holder is not a One Time Only holder and will require renewal certificates, check the Do they Receive Renewals? box. When you set up the delivery section, you can elect to have renewal certificates emailed to them automatically.
13
Enter the number of Cancellation Days
Enter the required number of cancellation notice days for this holder. In most cases this is 30 days.
14
Set Additional Insured and Waiver of Subrogation at the certificate line level
This is where you tell the certificate whether to include this holder as Additional Insured and/or Waiver of Subrogation at each coverage line. Set each line based on exactly what the holder's contract requires — no more, no less.
Example: If the holder needs to be named as Additional Insured on General Liability only and did not ask for Waiver of Subrogation on GL or Workers Comp: mark Y for GL Additional Insured, N for GL Subrogation Waiver, and N for Workers Comp Subrogation Waiver.
15
Review the Endorsements section and attach required documents
The Endorsements section controls which documents are attached with the certificate. If you marked Yes for Waiver of Subrogation or Additional Insured, include the corresponding endorsement documents here. Click View/Edit, find the correct documents in the list, and add them.
16
Review the Description of Operations — Certificate Portion
The Certificate Portion pulls from the template and applies to all holders. If wording appears here it is hardcoded from the template and cannot be edited at the holder level.
17
Complete the Description of Operations — Holder Specific Portion
The Holder Specific Portion is where you enter language specific to this holder. This text will auto-populate on all future certificates issued to this holder. Enter the agency-approved Additional Insured wording or any other required language here.
Use Library Lookup to access the agency's approved Description of Operations wording rather than typing it from scratch. Select the corresponding wording based on the certificate requirements.
18
Review the Delivery section options
The Delivery section allows you to send copies to up to three recipients. Important rules for this section:
  • A copy is automatically delivered to the Servicing Role CSR on file — an activity with the attachment is also created in Epic upon submission.
  • You can send delivery to up to 3 additional parties.
  • Do not enter the client's email here — delivery to the insured is controlled elsewhere in the account settings.
Send Me Confirmation: Unless you personally want an email copy, leave this unchecked — the CSR on file already receives one automatically.
19
Check "Supersedes Prior Certificate" if applicable
If this certificate replaces a previously issued certificate for this holder, select the Supersedes Prior Certificate option before submitting.
Phase 04 Delivery Recipient Setup, Submit & Epic Verification
20
Complete Recipient #1 — always the holder's delivery email
Enter the holder's delivery information in Recipient #1. Fill in the following fields:
  • Attention — name of the person you are sending to
  • Subject Line — keep vague and do not include a year, as this will be reused at renewal (e.g. Proof of Insurance for [Company Name])
  • Message — keep generic for the same reason; a professional template works well here
Always enter the holder's delivery email in Recipient #1. Because we set the holder to receive renewals and email those renewals, having the delivery email on file here maximizes future automation at renewal issuance time.
21
Select the email distribution checkboxes for Recipient #1
There are two email distribution options per recipient:
  • Email Current Certificate — sends the certificate being issued now
  • Email Renewal Cert — uses this same subject line, message, and email for all future certificates including renewals
When in doubt, check both. If the holder is One Time Only, you do not need to check Email Renewal Cert since they will date off and won't need a future certificate. For all others, always select Email Renewal Cert.
22
Enter the holder's email address for Recipient #1
Type the holder's email address in the Recipient #1 email field. Double-check the address before proceeding — a wrong address means the holder never receives the certificate.
23
Complete Recipient #2 and #3 if required
If additional parties need a copy, complete Recipient #2 and Recipient #3 following the same steps as Recipient #1 — attention, subject, message, distribution checkboxes, and email address.
24
Click "Preview"
Click Preview to download a PDF of the certificate as it will appear when issued. Review all fields — holder name and address, coverage limits, policy dates, AI/WVD selections, Description of Operations, and endorsement attachments — before submitting.
Preview is your last check. Once submitted, the certificate is a document of record. Correcting an error requires voiding the original and reissuing. Use Preview every time.
25
Click "Submit"
Click Submit to issue the certificate. Delivery information will display showing all recipients who were emailed copies. The certificate is now a document of record and updates are written back to Epic immediately upon submission.
26
Return to Epic and verify the activity was created
Go back to the account in Applied Epic and click Activities → All. Sort by Entered On Date to bring the most recent activity to the top. Look for the 4CER activity code — this is created automatically by CSR24 when it syncs the certificate to Epic.
27
Open the activity and access the certificate attachment
Highlight the 4CER activity, click Access, then click Attachments. The activity will filter and return a PDF copy of the issued certificate. Double-click to open and verify the certificate is correct.
Workflow complete. The certificate has been issued, delivered to all recipients, and a copy is now attached to the Epic activity record.
Common Questions
"Issue a Certificate" isn't showing — or it's routing to a request form. Why?▾

There are two common reasons. First, the insured may not have an active certificate template — if no template exists or all templates are expired (showing red in the template list), the system cannot issue directly and will route to a Certificate of Insurance Request form instead. Second, the insured may not have been granted issuance permission in CSR24. Check the template status first, and if a valid template is in place, review the insured's account permissions in the CSR24 utilities.

The policy limits on the certificate don't look right — what do I do?▾

CSR24 pulls policy data directly from Applied Epic via the data sync. If limits look wrong, the issue is almost certainly in the Epic policy record — not in CSR24. Cancel out, go to the policy in Applied Epic, confirm the limits are entered correctly, save, and then return to CSR24. The corrected data will pull through on the next sync (up to one hour). Do not manually override limits on the certificate — fix it at the source so Epic and CSR24 stay aligned.

I submitted with an error — can I edit the issued certificate?▾

Issued certificates cannot be edited in CSR24. To correct an error, void or supersede the original and issue a corrected replacement — which will carry a new certificate number. If the holder already received the original, notify them that a corrected version is coming and ask them to disregard the prior one. This is exactly why the Preview step exists — catching errors before submission avoids the voiding process entirely.

Does the insured automatically get a copy when I issue?▾

That depends on the account's CSR24 configuration. The Send a copy of all certificates to this insured setting controls whether the insured is automatically CC'd on every certificate issued. If it is on, the insured receives a copy every time. If this account also runs renewal batch processing, review that setting carefully before the batch — leaving it on during a batch run will send one email to the insured per holder, all at once, which can be a significant volume on large accounts.

Where do I find a certificate after it has been issued?▾

All issued certificates are accessible under Reprint Certificates in the Manage Certificates menu. Use the search to locate a specific certificate by holder name, date, or certificate number. From there you can view, reprint, or use the issued certificate as a starting point for a new issuance to the same holder.
